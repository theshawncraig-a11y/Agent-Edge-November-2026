Introducing Agent Accelerator

Branded Agent Websites, Built Right Into CINC

Your Whole Brand, in one place.

Between your brokerage’s site, your social profiles and everywhere else you show up online, your brand is probably a little spread out. Agent Accelerator gives you one place to bring it all together.

Agent Accelerator is a new one-page website option from CINC that gives you a personalized site of your own, built on the same platform as your broker’s main site. Every CINC platform includes at least one Agent Accelerator site at no cost. Your photo, bio and listings are already filled in, so you don’t have to start from scratch, and no design experience is required. Customize it yourself, or simply tell AI what you want to change.

You can create a draft and experiment until it feels just right. When you’re ready to go live, get a new domain right in setup or connect one you already own. Then publish it yourself or ask your broker to, depending on your team’s settings. Every inquiry from your site goes straight into your CRM, assigned to you.

Client Spotlight

40% of this team’s business still comes from CINC, 14 years later.

Susan Patton runs the Patton Group at RE/MAX Integrity in Tomball, Texas, with her husband and son. She inherited a CINC account when her mentor retired — and 14 years on, CINC leads and repeat CINC clients still drive roughly 40% of the team’s income.

01

Signed-up leads are not cold leads.

From January through June, seven of the team’s closings came from CINC leads — $3,342,900 in sales volume. Two were repeat CINC clients worth $1,708,000. As Susan puts it: “I don’t consider it to be a cold lead platform, because they’ve signed up.”

02

The dialer and transcriber do the heavy lifting.

Susan loads 50 to 100 numbers into the dialer and lets it run, pausing to add notes as she goes. The call transcriber captures search criteria from conversations she takes on the road, so nothing gets lost before the follow-up.

03

Nurture like a crock pot, not a microwave.

A relocation client from New Mexico became two transactions totaling $1,570,000 five years apart. A lead who sat on the platform for three years finally bought with her sister because Susan kept calling to check in, not to sell. Her advice: the clients you nurture over time are the loyal ones who bring repeat business.
